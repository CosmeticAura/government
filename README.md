# Government and Economics study site

Study loops for the Foundations of American Democracy summative at The Clinton School: the essay format and rubric, the 14 key terms, a full plan for each of the three prompts, and the post-it skeletons.

Live site: https://cosmeticaura.github.io/government/

- `unit-1/` runs each section as a loop: read and take Cornell notes, a 5 minute break, flashcards where they help, another break, a brain dump, then mark what you missed. `?s=p1` opens a section directly.
- `practice/` is a 40 minute timed essay with a prompt picker, autosave, a word count and a rubric self check that counts your key terms.
- `terms/` is the key terms sheet in the three columns of the prep sheet, ready to print or copy by hand.

Progress saves in your browser only. Rebuild the loop pages with the studynow kit: `python3 build.py course.py OUT`, then copy `index.html` and `unit-1/` back.

- `diagnostic/` checks facts, terms, essay format and your three theses.
